## Multiple Choice


### MCQ 1
How many digits in binary?

- A) 0 and 1 only  
- B) 0–9  
- C) 0–15  
- D) A–F  

**Answer: A**

### MCQ 2
One byte equals how many bits?

- A) 8  
- B) 4  
- C) 16  
- D) 10  

**Answer: A**

### MCQ 3
Decimal 5 in binary is:

- A) 101  
- B) 110  
- C) 111  
- D) 100  

**Answer: A**

### MCQ 4
Why do computers use binary?

- A) Hardware switches ON/OFF  
- B) Humans prefer it  
- C) Smaller fonts  
- D) Random choice  

**Answer: A**

### MCQ 5
Bit means:

- A) Binary digit  
- B) Byte in transit  
- C) Big integer type  
- D) Boot flag  

**Answer: A**
